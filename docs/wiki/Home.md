## TerminalStyles

Switch terminal themes live. Run `tstyles`, arrow through the styles with each
one previewing in your current tab, press **Enter** to keep it or **Esc** to put
the terminal back exactly as it was.

The [README](https://github.com/fcreme/TerminalStyles#readme) has the short
version. This wiki has the rest.
