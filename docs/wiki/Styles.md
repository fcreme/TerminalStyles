## Styles

Eighteen themes ship out of the box. Click any name to jump to that style's
folder for full palette / prompt / theme.json details.

### [umbrella](https://github.com/fcreme/TerminalStyles/tree/main/styles/umbrella)

![umbrella](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fcreme/TerminalStyles/main/docs/screenshots/umbrella.png)

**Resident-Evil / survival-horror.** Blood-red brackets, bone-white text,
three-line classified-doc prompt with a startup banner.
*"Welcome to Umbrella Corporation. Status: FINE."*

### [phosphor](https://github.com/fcreme/TerminalStyles/tree/main/styles/phosphor)

![phosphor](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fcreme/TerminalStyles/main/docs/screenshots/phosphor.png)

**A green phosphor tube.** Twelve of the sixteen ANSI slots are one green at
different brightnesses, because that is all a P1 tube could do — the four
that are not are amber, for the alarm a green screen could not paint.
*"READY."*

### [skyline](https://github.com/fcreme/TerminalStyles/tree/main/styles/skyline)

![skyline](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fcreme/TerminalStyles/main/docs/screenshots/skyline.png)

**A balcony over the city at night.** Saturated indigo, lit windows, and a
strip of green signage along the far bank — the one bright colour in the
frame, so it is the cursor.
*"The city keeps its own hours."*

### [koholint](https://github.com/fcreme/TerminalStyles/tree/main/styles/koholint)

![koholint](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fcreme/TerminalStyles/main/docs/screenshots/koholint.png)

**Link's Awakening, adrift off Koholint.** GBC sea blue and cloud white,
with the hat band as the only yellow on screen — so it is the cursor and
nothing else.
*"The Wind Fish sleeps long."*

### [eva](https://github.com/fcreme/TerminalStyles/tree/main/styles/eva)

![eva](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fcreme/TerminalStyles/main/docs/screenshots/eva.png)

**Evangelion / Asuka body-scan.** Coral-red CRT palette with mustard
yellow status overlay. NERV operations banner.
*"Anta baka?"*

### [ex-machina](https://github.com/fcreme/TerminalStyles/tree/main/styles/ex-machina)

![ex-machina](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fcreme/TerminalStyles/main/docs/screenshots/ex-machina.png)

**Ava body-scan / Bluebook research.** Cold electric cyan wireframe
with a coral pink accent.
*"You've been programmed and fed by another. Are you bothered?"*

### [forest](https://github.com/fcreme/TerminalStyles/tree/main/styles/forest)

![forest](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fcreme/TerminalStyles/main/docs/screenshots/forest.png)

**Quiet alpine wilderness.** Pixel-art mountain vista — snow-tipped
peaks at golden hour, deep evergreen forest, mirror lake. Cool
blue-green base, peach accents. No banner — gentle for long sessions.

### [garden-rain](https://github.com/fcreme/TerminalStyles/tree/main/styles/garden-rain)

![garden-rain](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fcreme/TerminalStyles/main/docs/screenshots/garden-rain.png)

**Ghibli-style garden rainfall.** Wet stone steps, a metal bucket
and a blue plastic one catching rainwater, leafy plants with blue
flowers. Cool slate-blue base, plant-green cursor, no banner.

### [gitbash](https://github.com/fcreme/TerminalStyles/tree/main/styles/gitbash)

![gitbash](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fcreme/TerminalStyles/main/docs/screenshots/gitbash.png)

**Git Bash / MinTTY recreation.** White background, near-black text,
bar cursor, the classic multi-color prompt with live git-branch
detection. The only light-mode theme in the catalog.

### [golden-forest](https://github.com/fcreme/TerminalStyles/tree/main/styles/golden-forest)

![golden-forest](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fcreme/TerminalStyles/main/docs/screenshots/golden-forest.png)

**Warm sepia autumn.** Amber and moss palette over deep dark green.
Quiet — no banner, gentle for long sessions.

### [kitty](https://github.com/fcreme/TerminalStyles/tree/main/styles/kitty)

![kitty](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fcreme/TerminalStyles/main/docs/screenshots/kitty.png)

### [lain](https://github.com/fcreme/TerminalStyles/tree/main/styles/lain)

### [marquee](https://github.com/fcreme/TerminalStyles/tree/main/styles/marquee)

![marquee](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fcreme/TerminalStyles/main/docs/screenshots/marquee.png)

**EXT3-series LED marquee.** Hot magenta dot-matrix portrait,

*"Through the glass, after hours."*

### [neon-rain](https://github.com/fcreme/TerminalStyles/tree/main/styles/neon-rain)

### [rain](https://github.com/fcreme/TerminalStyles/tree/main/styles/rain)

![rain](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fcreme/TerminalStyles/main/docs/screenshots/rain.png)

**Contemplative highland-storm.** Slate-purple stormy sky, moss-yellow

### [tombraider](https://github.com/fcreme/TerminalStyles/tree/main/styles/tombraider)

![tombraider](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fcreme/TerminalStyles/main/docs/screenshots/tombraider.png)

**90s arcade marquee.** LED-pixel TOMB RAIDER sign in hot neon
magenta with Lara's silhouette, gold EXT3 label, cyan highlights.
Pure black base, neon magenta cursor, vintage CRT cursor.
*"Press start."*

---

Most screenshots are generated by `scripts/capture-screenshots.ps1` (see
[Adding your own style](Creating-a-Style.md)). If they're missing or out of date, run
that script from inside Windows Terminal and commit the output.

Some are **renders** drawn by `scripts/make-preview.py` instead, and each
says so in its style README. `capture-screenshots.ps1` writes every theme,
so it will replace those with captures — regenerate a render with
`python3 scripts/make-preview.py <style>` and commit only the files you
meant to change. `tests/Screenshot-Provenance.Tests.ps1` fails if a render
and its note disagree.
