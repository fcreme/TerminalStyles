# halo

Third in the EXT3 LED-marquee series, sister piece to tombraider
and marquee. Pixel-art psychedelic portrait inside a circular halo
ring, rendered in coral, magenta, lavender, blue, and gold dot-matrix
LEDs against pure black. Coral-orange cursor and tab color, arcade
gold strings, electric blue variables, vintage CRT cursor.

Right-aligned image on a pure-black canvas — text on the left, halo
glowing from the right edge. *"Look up. Look closer."*
