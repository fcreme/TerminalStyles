# marquee

EXT3-series LED marquee, sister piece to tombraider. Pixel-art
portrait of a girl in hot magenta with cool blue arm accents and
the gold "EXT3" call-out label, dot-matrix rendering against pure
black. Magenta cursor and tab color, arcade gold strings, electric
blue variables, vintage CRT cursor.

Right-aligned image on a pure-black canvas — text sits on the left
half, portrait glows from the right edge. Arcade-marquee banner.
*"Through the glass, after hours."*
